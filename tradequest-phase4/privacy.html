Privacy Policy

Launch-draft document. Have qualified counsel review these terms before accepting paying customers.

Effective draft: September 21, 2026

Data we expect to process

Production TradeQuest may process account details, authentication identifiers, lesson progress, virtual portfolio activity, subscription status, device/security logs and messages sent to the educational AI Coach.

Why we use it

Data is used to provide accounts, preserve progress, operate simulations, process subscriptions, secure the service, support users and improve the learning experience.

Service providers

The Phase 4 architecture can use Supabase for authentication/data, Stripe for billing and OpenAI for the educational Coach. Their production configuration and final disclosures should be reviewed before launch.

Your choices

The production service should provide mechanisms to access, correct and delete account data as required by applicable law. Marketing communications should include appropriate opt-out controls.

Before launch

Add the operating company's legal identity, contact method, retention periods, cookie/analytics details, state-specific disclosures where applicable, and procedures for minors before accepting public registrations.
